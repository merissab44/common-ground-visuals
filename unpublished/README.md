# Unpublished images

Photos here are not served by the site (only `public/` is). The Portraits
collection is paused until permission to publish is confirmed.

To bring it back:
1. Move the files to `public/images/portraits/` and `public/images/community/cook-red-hat.jpg`.
2. Remove `hidden: true` from the portraits entry in `src/data/collections.ts`.
